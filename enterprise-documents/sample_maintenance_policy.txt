Facility Equipment Maintenance Policy

1. Overview
This document outlines the standard maintenance procedures for HVAC and elevator
systems across all DAMAC managed properties.

2. HVAC Maintenance Schedule
HVAC units must be inspected on a quarterly basis. Filters should be replaced every
90 days. Refrigerant levels must be checked twice a year, in March and September.

3. Elevator Maintenance Schedule
Elevators require monthly safety inspections performed by a certified technician.
Any elevator that records more than 3 unscheduled stops in a single month must be
taken out of service and inspected within 24 hours. Cable tension checks are required
every 6 months.

4. Predictive Maintenance Guidelines
Facilities equipped with IoT vibration and temperature sensors should flag any
deviation greater than 15% from the equipment baseline. When flagged, maintenance
staff have 48 hours to investigate before escalation to the regional facilities manager.
Historical data shows catching deviations early reduces unplanned downtime by 30-35%.

5. Vendor Response Times
For HVAC emergencies, the vendor must respond within 4 hours.
For elevator emergencies, response time must not exceed 1 hour.

6. Reporting
All maintenance activity must be logged within 24 hours of completion.
